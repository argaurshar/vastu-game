The Nine Zones

Every placement rule flows from the Pancha Bhoota — the five elements seated in the mandala: Water in the North-East, Fire in the South-East, Earth in the South-West, Air in the North-West and Space at the centre. Match a room's activity to its zone's element and the plan works; oppose it (fire in the water corner, a void in the earth corner) and you create a dosha. Click any zone on the map above for its rooms and interior palette.

Main Entrance Planner — 32 Padas

The outer ring of the 9×9 Vastu Purusha Mandala divides the boundary into 32 padas, eight per side, each ruled by a deity. Divide the length of each outer wall into eight equal parts (measured clockwise, N and S walls west→east, E and W walls north→south on this chart) and place the main door on an auspicious pada. Quick Reference

  • North: N3 Mukhya, N4 Bhallata, N5 Soma — wealth and recognition.

  • West: W3 Sugriva, W4 Pushpadanta — gains and fulfilment; W5 Varuna acceptable.
  • South: only S4 Grihakshata is workable; all other South padas are avoided.
  • Corner padas (E1, S1, W1, N1) are always avoided.
